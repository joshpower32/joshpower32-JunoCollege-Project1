Golden State

California · 6 min read

Two weeks, one rented car, and the entire California coast laid out like a promise. We started in the fog of San Francisco and chased the sun south until it finally caught us somewhere past Big Sur.

The Golden State earns its name slowly — in the late-afternoon light on the cliffs, in the dry gold hills inland, in the glow of a cheap motel sign that somehow felt like home for a night.

11 Cities in 12 Days

Europe · 7 min read

It was the kind of plan that sounds reckless out loud: eleven cities, twelve days, a single backpack, and a rail pass that never stopped feeling like a magic trick. Some mornings I woke up genuinely unsure which country I was in.

You don't really see a city in a day. But you can fall a little in love with one — a courtyard in Prague, a bakery in Lisbon, a train window somewhere over the Alps — and that turned out to be more than enough.

Year in Awe

Reflections · 4 min read

This one isn't a single trip. It's a whole year, stitched together from departure gates, window seats, and the strange quiet of arriving somewhere new after dark.

Looking back, the highlights weren't the famous views. They were the small, unrepeatable moments in between — and the slow realization that wonder is less about where you go and more about how closely you're willing to look.
